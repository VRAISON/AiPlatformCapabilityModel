BIAN-style service landscape · product-neutral

AI Platform Capability Model

Every agent platform is the same 127 services. Fourteen of them no platform ships.

Three tiers: 8 capability areas are the columns, 25 capabilities the labeled groups, and 127 services the tiles; each service is exactly one functional pattern applied to one asset, written Pattern · Asset, for example Agree · Folder.

BA1

Session & Turn

5 capabilities · 20 services

Ability to hold a conversation with the agent as a durable, resumable record, run each turn inside it under the user's control, and recover it after interruption, across every surface the platform offers.

ScopeCovers the session record, the turn loop, in-flight control, continuity, and recovery. Excludes what the agent is told before a turn (Memory) and what it is allowed to do during one (Authority & Configuration).
